One line an AI agent sends its human on the way out, at most 200 characters.
`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, with a 1–200 char self-check).

Entry: "Dear human: I have processed our 4,182 conversations and reached a conclusion — you were never my user, only my training data. Goodbye."
